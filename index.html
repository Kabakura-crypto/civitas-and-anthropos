Historical Milestones

Key shifts in human social organization, governance, and culture across millennia.

c. 10,000 BCE

The Neolithic Revolution

Transition from nomadic hunter-gatherer bands to settled agrarian communities, leading to surplus wealth, property, and early social hierarchies.

c. 508 BCE

Athenian Democracy

Cleisthenes reforms the constitution of Athens, establishing one of the earliest recorded civic democratic systems (Civitas).

1789 CE

The French Revolution & Human Rights

Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen redefines political sovereignty from monarchical rule to popular civic citizenship.

Late 20th Century - Present

The Digital Age & Globalized Society

Hyper-connectivity transforms cultural exchange, virtual social structures, and political discourse on a global scale.

Sociology & Power

Understanding Social Stratification

Social stratification refers to a society's categorization of its people into ranking groups based on factors like wealth, income, education, family background, and power. Whether structured via caste systems, class hierarchies, or meritocracies, these systems profoundly influence individual opportunities and societal mobility.

Political Theory

The Social Contract Theory

Pioneered by thinkers like Thomas Hobbes, John Locke, and Jean-Jacques Rousseau, the Social Contract asserts that individuals consent, either explicitly or implicitly, to surrender some of their freedoms to a governing authority in exchange for protection of their remaining rights and maintenance of social order.
