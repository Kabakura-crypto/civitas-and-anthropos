CIVITAS & ANTHROPOS

Culture, Society, & Politics — By Grace Albancia

Core Learning Modules

Explore the foundational pillars connecting human identity, social organization, and governance systems.

1. Anthropology

The study of human origins, biological variations, and cultural evolution. Learn how rituals, symbols, and beliefs define the human story.

2. Sociology

Investigating social institutions, group dynamics, stratification, and systemic interactions that shape human behavior across groups.

3. Political Science

Understanding state power, citizenship responsibilities, governance structures, rights, and political behavior within society.
